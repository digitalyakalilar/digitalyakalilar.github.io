Use is not trust. Use is opening the tool. Trust is treating the output as correct.

PwC’s **Global Workforce Hopes and Fears Survey 2026** measured that split. It was published on 26 October 2026. About 50,000 workers took part worldwide. The page does not give a separate Türkiye count [1]. Do not read the percentages as the whole country.

In Türkiye, 75 percent expect AI use at work to rise over the next 12 months. Inside that, 25 percent say it will rise, 31 percent say it will rise moderately, and 19 percent say it will rise a lot. The global figure is 60 percent [1]. Those who expect little change are 22 percent in Türkiye and 29 percent globally [1].

An expectation is not today’s use.

## A daily tool and a daily agent are separate

A generative tool writes a text or a summary. An agent takes a step in a person’s place. Adding the two rates does not make a new rate.

| Item | Türkiye | Global |
|---|---|---|
| Expect a rise within 12 months | 75 percent | 60 percent |
| Daily generative tool | 34 percent | 22 percent |
| Daily agent | 16 percent | 10 percent |
| Accuracy blocks more use | 27 percent | 31 percent of daily users |
| Fear that the job is replaced | 25 percent | 15 percent |
| Not enough chance to use it | 24 percent | not stated |
| Policy is unclear | 21 percent | 15 percent |
| Lack of skill | 8 percent | 15 percent |

Daily generative use is 34 percent in Türkiye and 22 percent globally. Weekly use is 19 percent on both sides [1]. Daily agent use is 16 percent in Türkiye and 10 percent globally. People who never use an agent are 34 percent in Türkiye and 48 percent globally [1].

## The barrier is not ignorance

A barrier is the reason more use stops.

In Türkiye the first reason is accuracy and quality, at 27 percent. Then fear of replacement at 25 percent, no chance to use it at 24 percent, and an unclear policy at 21 percent [1]. Lack of skill is 8 percent. Globally, lack of skill is 15 percent [1].

One example. A person drafts a job ad with the tool. If HR publishes it without a check, the error becomes the ad. If HR checks it, the tool stays a draft. The 27 percent is the missing check.

Daily users are more selective. Globally, about a third of them, 31 percent, name accuracy as the first barrier. That is 6 points above the global average [1]. The person who uses the tool often sees the error more clearly.

Fear of job loss is 25 percent in Türkiye and 15 percent globally [1]. The share who expect their role to disappear within three years is 5 percent. Globally it is 3 percent [1]. The fear is larger than the expectation. Reading the fear as “most people will lose their jobs” turns 5 percent into 25 percent.

## One company, two speeds

PwC splits the workforce into four groups. Pioneers are 14 percent. The core workforce is 56 percent [1]. Daily generative use is 51 percent among pioneers and 11 percent in the core. Most of the core say they have never used it [1].

The sentence “the company uses AI” hides the two speeds. The few who use it and the many who do not hear the same policy.

The conclusion is plain. The tool is open. Whether the output counts as correct is unwritten. Use does not become trust there.

## Checklist

- [ ] Is it written where a generative tool is allowed, and where an agent is allowed?
- [ ] May candidate, customer, or employee data enter the tool?
- [ ] Which output must a person check? Accuracy is the first barrier, at 27 percent [1].
- [ ] Can the user find the policy? An unclear policy is a barrier for 21 percent [1].
- [ ] Does the core workforce get a chance, or does use stay with the pioneers?
- [ ] Is “you will lose your job” being mixed with the 5 percent who expect the role to go?

## Frequently asked questions

### How many people in Türkiye were surveyed?

PwC reports about 50,000 workers worldwide [1]. The 26 October 2026 page does not give a Türkiye count. The percentage is the Türkiye break. It is not the population.

### Do 75 percent use AI today?

No. Seventy-five percent expect use to rise within 12 months [1]. Daily generative use is 34 percent. Daily agent use is 16 percent [1].

### Is lack of skill the first barrier?

No. Accuracy is first, at 27 percent. Lack of skill is 8 percent [1].

### Why keep shadow use separate?

A worker can bring the tool. An unclear policy is a barrier for 21 percent in Türkiye and 15 percent globally [1]. Without a policy, where the data goes is also unwritten.
