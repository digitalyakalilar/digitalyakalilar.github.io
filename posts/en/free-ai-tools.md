AI tools are no longer just toys for tech enthusiasts. With the right tool, emails, reports and research can take minutes instead of hours. Here are 5 tools you can start using for free:

## 1. Grok
Follows what's happening on X in real time, answers questions and generates images. Ideal for quickly summarizing the latest news in your industry.

## 2. ChatGPT
Email drafts, meeting summaries, presentation ideas... Explain clearly what you want and it will have a first draft ready in seconds.

## 3. Google Gemini
Summarizes long texts and helps you write. With your Google account, it's easy to use alongside Gmail and Docs.

## 4. Perplexity
Researches with sources. Make it your first stop when you want to see where a piece of information comes from.

## 5. NotebookLM
Answers questions from the PDFs and notes you upload, and can even create audio summaries. Perfect for digesting long reports and training materials.

> Tip: Before pasting confidential company information into these tools, check your company's AI policy.
