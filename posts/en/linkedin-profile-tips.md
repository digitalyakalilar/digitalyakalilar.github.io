LinkedIn is the digital business card for white-collar professionals, yet most profiles are only half finished. Set aside 15 minutes and work through these steps:

## 1. Write a clear headline
Don't just list your job title. Use a headline that shows what you do and where your expertise lies, like "Finance Specialist | Budget Planning & Reporting | Excel & Power BI".

## 2. A professional photo and cover image
Choose a photo where your face is clearly visible against a simple background, and a cover image that reflects your industry.

## 3. Turn your "About" section into a story
In 3–4 short paragraphs, explain what you do, the results you've achieved and what interests you. Use numbers.

## 4. Add achievements to your experience
Write results instead of a task list, for example: "Cut reporting time by 40%."

## 5. Update your skills and recommendations
Add the skills that appear in job postings, and ask people you've worked with for short recommendations.

## 6. Post and comment regularly
One short post or thoughtful comment a week can significantly boost your visibility. Weekday mornings usually work well for posting.
